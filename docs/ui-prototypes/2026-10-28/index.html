Four ways to give Persona 4 Golden its own skin

Nocturne got COMP and P5R got Calling Card. P4G will be the third game, so it needs a third look that feels like Inaba rather than either of those. All four use the same real screen: the fusion calculator with Angel + Anzu making Jack Frost, plus a short persona list. The data comes from the same upstream source and commit the importer already pins.

Every direction keeps the app dark-only and uses P4G yellow as the game accent. There is no game switch this time, because each skin is made for P4G only.

Midnight Channel

Personality from the medium

The result is shown on an old TV set in a dark room while it rains outside. The set switches on with a white line and a burst of static, then a yellow "Now showing" ticker slides in and states the fusion rule. The parents are tuned channels and the list is the TV listings.

Porting effort: medium. Most of the work is tokens and a condensed display face. It needs one new TV-frame component around the result and a small static canvas. PrimeNG controls only need pill radii and colour.

Junes

Personality from voice

The page is the department store's weekly flyer, with a red awning, price-tag levels and a "Today's fusion special". A price-gun starburst slaps the level onto the result. Stats come in a nutrition-label box, and affinities are written out in a "Handle with care" line.

Porting effort: medium-high. The paper panels can reuse Calling Card's paper-token flip. It needs a sticker component and voice copy for every page title and empty state, and a second heavy display font eats into the 500 kB budget.

Case Board

Personality from a domain metaphor

The Investigation Team's corkboard. The parents are pinned index cards, and red yarn is strung from their pins to a photo of the result. A handwritten note works out the level rule, weaknesses are circled in red pen and highlighted, and the list is a typed sheet.

Porting effort: high. The yarn is an SVG overlay measured from live element positions, so it needs a ResizeObserver service. Tilted cards fight dense pages, so lists stay flat. Three new fonts are too many, and one would have to go.

Fog

Personality from atmosphere and the pointer

A quiet, light-weight page standing in drifting yellow-grey fog. It opens fogged in, and the fog lifts outward from the result's name. On desktop your pointer becomes a pair of glasses that clears the fog where you look. A yellow lens ring frames the result.

Porting effort: low-medium. It adds one fog layer and the pointer lens in the shell, and the components barely change. Panels need backdrop blur, and the fog has to pause when off-screen and under reduced motion.

Mixing is fair game. Each direction is built from separate parts: a palette, a typeface, one signature element, and a tone of voice. You could put the Midnight Channel TV on the Fog page as the result frame, or use the Case Board's highlighter and red-pen circles for weaknesses in any of the others.

These are mockups. The hovers, the search filter, the mode buttons and each page's opening moment all work: the TV switching on, the sticker landing, the yarn being strung and the fog lifting. No fusion logic is wired. The recipe was worked out from the upstream P4G chart with the normal level rule, not by the app's engine.
